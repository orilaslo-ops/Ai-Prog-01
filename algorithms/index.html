Complex Algorithms

The algorithms that look like magic on the whiteboard, slowed down until every decision makes sense. Every page here works the same way:

  1. The problemWhat it solves, in plain English, before any code.
  2. The animationWatch the algorithm work, one step at a time.
  3. Live stateIndexes, tables, stacks and counters as they change.
  4. The codeShort pseudocode with the running line highlighted.
  5. The whyEach step explains the reason for the decision, not just the move.
  6. Your inputChange the size or values, or randomize, and run it again.
  7. Predict the next stepPause, guess what happens next, then see if you were right.
